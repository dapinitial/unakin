## 2. Client classes

Every client is assigned a class. The class sets the **courtesy**, not the quality of work — all clients receive the same senior craft and the same contracts.

| Class | Who | Courtesy off rack |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial** | Corporations, funded businesses | None. Full rack. |
| **Startup / SMB** | Small funded teams | Discretionary, capped, time-boxed. |
| **Player Rate** ("the rope team") | Friends, fellow climbers & creators — *invite-only* | Sliding-scale courtesy, itemized. |
| **Nonprofit / Mission** | Registered nonprofits, causes | Reduced rate or pro-bono allocation. |
| **Barter / Trade** | Value-for-value partners | Full or partial trade at FMV. ⚠️ |

⚠️ **Barter is taxable.** Goods or services received in trade (e.g. a guided expedition, gear) are income at fair market value, and what you provide is likewise valued. Both sides get documented on the SOW and reported. **CPA review required** before relying on any barter arrangement.

## 3. How a courtesy appears (the mechanism)

On every proposal and invoice for a discounted client:

```
Immersive 3D fly-by build (rack) .................. $32,000
  Player Rate courtesy (friend of the studio) ..... –$[ COURTESY ]
Your investment ................................... $[ FRIEND-FAIR TOTAL ]
```

The recipient sees the true value *and* the gift. Future commercial clients see only rack. You never appear to undersell.

## 5. Discounts & policy notes

- Courtesies are **granted, not negotiated**, and are extended at the studio's discretion.
- A courtesy applies to a specific SOW; it does not set a precedent for future work.
- Rush work (compressed timelines, weekend/holiday delivery) may carry a rush multiplier, quoted per engagement.
- The studio retains a perpetual license to reusable components, libraries, and techniques it develops (see IP Assignment & License).
