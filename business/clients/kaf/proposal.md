## What we'll build

### Core build & go-live
The immersive experience, made production-grade and running on your infrastructure:

- **Production hardening** of the 3D fly-by app — performance across devices, graceful loading, mobile behavior, error handling, analytics hooks.
- **Design & creative direction pass** — the polish that separates a demo from a signature piece: motion, pacing, typography, the kaf brand woven in.
- **Infrastructure provisioning & deployment** — stand up **kaf-owned** Supabase + DigitalOcean (billed to kaf), deploy the app and your data there, wire the domain.
- **Go-live & operations setup** — credentials and data under kaf's control, a written runbook, analytics wired in, and a live walkthrough. Unakin operates it for you thereafter (see managed care).

### Optional module — White-label embed CMS
So kaf publishes routes without calling us:

- A branded admin where your team adds/edits routes, uploads GPX/imagery, and tunes the fly-through.
- A single **embed snippet** that drops the experience into Squarespace, Shopify, or anywhere else — styled as kaf, not us.
- Multi-route library so the experience scales across your whole catalog.

### Managed care (recommended)
Unakin deploys, updates, and operates the experience for you — new routes, hosting oversight, security patches, priority support — so you get the outcome without running a 3D engine in-house. Because everything lives on *your* accounts, this is about convenience and expertise on tap, not lock-in (details below).

## Ownership, hosting & continuity

Here's exactly who owns and controls what — in plain English:

- **Your data, brand, and content are 100% yours.** Routes, imagery, copy, the kaf brand — owned outright by kaf.
- **It runs on your own accounts.** Supabase + DigitalOcean provisioned under kaf, billed to kaf, credentials in your hands. Your experience stays live independent of Unakin's infrastructure — **no hosting lock-in.**
- **Unakin owns the software; kaf gets a perpetual license to use it.** The underlying source code and the reusable 3D fly-through engine remain Unakin's property. kaf receives a **perpetual, non-exclusive license** to run the delivered application for kaf's business. This is what keeps your investment down — you're buying a finished, running experience and expert operation of it, not a codebase to maintain.
- **Continuity, guaranteed.** Because the app and data live on your accounts and your license is perpetual, kaf keeps running the delivered build no matter what. If we ever part ways, a written transition plan (and, if you like, source escrow) ensures you're never stranded.
- **Unakin operates it for you** under the managed-care plan — so you get the outcome without running a 3D engine in-house.
