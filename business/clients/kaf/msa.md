# Master Services Agreement

> ⚖️ **DRAFT for attorney review before use.** Plain-English working draft — not legal advice.

This Master Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of {{today}} ("**Effective Date**") between **{{studio.legalName}}**, a {{studio.state}} {{studio.entityType}} ("**{{studio.dba}}**", "we", "us"), and **{{client.legalName}}** ("**Client**", "you"). {{studio.dba}} and Client are each a "Party."

## 1. Structure
This Agreement sets the general terms for all work. Each engagement is described in a separate **Statement of Work ("SOW")** that references this Agreement. If a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, the SOW controls **for that engagement only**.

## 2. Services
{{studio.dba}} will perform the services and provide the deliverables ("**Deliverables**") described in each SOW, with professional skill and care.

## 3. Fees & payment
- Fees are stated in each SOW, derived from {{studio.dba}}'s rate card (standard rate ${{rates.rackHourly}}/hr {{rates.currency}}; specialized ${{rates.specializedHourly}}/hr).
- A **{{rates.depositPct}}% deposit** is due on signature before work begins, and is non-refundable once work starts.
- Invoices are due **Net {{rates.paymentTermsDays}}**. Overdue amounts accrue **{{rates.lateFeeMonthlyPct}}% per month**. {{studio.dba}} may pause work on any invoice {{rates.paymentTermsDays}}+ days overdue.
- Pre-approved pass-through costs (hosting, domains, third-party licenses, assets) are billed at cost.
- All amounts are in {{rates.currency}} and exclusive of taxes. Payment is made to **{{studio.legalName}}**.

## 4. Intellectual property & license  ⚖️
This is the core commercial term — **have counsel confirm it matches each SOW.**

- **4.1 {{studio.dba}} retains ownership.** {{studio.dba}} owns all right, title, and interest in the source code, software, tools, frameworks, and the reusable engine and techniques used or created in performing the Services (collectively, the "**Studio IP**"), including anything developed before or independently of this engagement.
- **4.2 License to Client.** On **full payment** for a given SOW, {{studio.dba}} grants Client a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use, run, and display the delivered solution** for Client's own business. Client may not resell, sublicense, or distribute the Studio IP, or use the reusable engine to build competing products.
- **4.3 Client Content & Data.** Client owns all of its own content, branding, and data. Client grants {{studio.dba}} a limited license to use them only to perform the Services.
- **4.4 Optional source assignment.** If Client wishes to **own** the source code, the Parties may agree in a separate signed writing to a source-code assignment or buyout at a price reflecting the transfer of Studio IP.
- **4.5 Feedback.** {{studio.dba}} may freely use general knowledge, skills, and feedback gained during the engagement.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each Party will protect the other's non-public information, use it only for this engagement, and not disclose it, for **[3] years** after disclosure. Excludes information that is public, independently developed, or lawfully obtained.

## 6. Warranty & disclaimer
{{studio.dba}} warrants the Deliverables will materially conform to the SOW for **[30] days** after acceptance; sole remedy is re-performance. Otherwise the Deliverables are provided **"as is,"** and {{studio.dba}} disclaims all other warranties to the extent permitted by law.

## 7. Limitation of liability  ⚖️
Except for a Party's confidentiality breach or indemnity obligations, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, and each Party's **total liability is capped at the fees paid by Client under the applicable SOW in the [12] months before the claim.**

## 8. Term & termination
Either Party may terminate an engagement on **[15] days'** written notice. On termination, Client pays for work performed through the termination date. Sections 3–7, 9 survive.

## 9. General
- **9.1 Independent contractor.** {{studio.dba}} is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client.
- **9.2 Governing law.** The laws of the State of **{{studio.state}}**, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. Venue lies in the state or federal courts located in {{studio.state}}.
- **9.3 Entire agreement.** This Agreement plus its SOWs is the entire agreement and supersedes prior discussions. Changes must be in a signed writing.
- **9.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign without the other's consent, except in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
- **9.5 Counterparts & e-signature.** May be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each deemed an original.
